# 60-day momentum lever — real but fragile, exit-conditional

`BULLISH & mom_60 ≥ +0.35 & |delta| 0.20–0.46` on a **3-day hold** survived adversarial
testing: +14.2%/day, bootstrap CI [+4.3%, +24.3%], 2 of 3 skeptics. But it is the single
survivor of ~16 interaction-mined candidates, the effect decayed +16% → +1.7% on the
newest slice, and it has **zero edge under the same-day GIGO exit**. A forward 3-day label
arm is accruing confirmation data; treat N as insufficient until it lands.

Application: **proposer-only.** May be cited as a tilt toward a candidate ONLY if the
planned hold is multi-day; citing it for a same-day trade is a trade-critic FAIL (the
canonical exit-context mismatch). Depends on [[delta-band-0-20-0-46]] as its conditioning
variable.
