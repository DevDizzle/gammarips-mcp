# 10–15% OTM was the best moneyness bucket

Moneyness around **10–15% OTM** outperformed both nearer-the-money and deeper-OTM buckets
on realized option PnL. Consistent with [[delta-band-0-20-0-46]] (the bands overlap
heavily for typical vol/DTE).

Application: a secondary check on candidates — inside 10–15% OTM is a plus; far outside
needs a reason. Prefer the delta band when the two disagree (delta is the better-measured
lever).
