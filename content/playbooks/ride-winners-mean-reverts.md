# "Ride recent winners" — falsified

Tickers/contracts that just produced winning option trades did NOT persist — recent-winner
persistence tested as an **anti-edge** (mean reversion) on our cohort.

Application: a candidate being "hot lately" is not support; if anything it's mild
counter-evidence. Do not let a recent journal win on the same ticker bias today's
selection.
